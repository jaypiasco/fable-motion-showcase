## Brand & Style

The design system projects an authoritative, high-craft creative suite for next-generation cinematic AI video production. Rooted in an ultra-deep obsidian workspace, the aesthetic evokes high-end virtual production monitors, color-grading environments, and generative AI command centers. 

### Visual Philosophy
- **Cinematic Submersion:** The canvas recedes into near-absolute darkness so high-frame-rate video renders, latent canvas outputs, and generative timelines step into intense focus.
- **Electric Precision:** Neon ultraviolet, electric cyan, and laser magenta communicate computational speed, model intelligence, and rendering state.
- **Refined Translucency:** Structural boundaries rely on micro-thin frosted glassmorphic dividers, glowing hairline borders, and dark crystalline surfaces rather than solid opaque fills.

### Target Audience & Emotional Tone
Designed for creative directors, visual effects artists, AI filmmakers, and technical creatives who demand professional power tools. The experience balances intense technical capability with sleek, effortless precision—evoking hyper-focus, infinite creative headroom, and studio-grade confidence.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered specifically for darkened creative suites, prioritizing contrast against rendering viewports while preventing optical fatigue during extended generation workflows.

### Chromatic Roles
- **Obsidian Dark Grounds:** `#07050D` serves as the canvas substrate, paired with `#0F0C1B` for elevated studio panels, dock shelves, and timeline tracks.
- **Primary Violet/Neon Purple (`#8B5CF6`, `#7C3AED`):** The engine color. Drives primary interactions, progress arcs, timeline playheads, and AI render states.
- **Secondary Cyan/Azure (`#06B6D4`, `#3B82F6`):** Precision cues, frame scrubbing cursors, audio-reactive track meters, and parameter adjustment sliders.
- **Tertiary Magenta (`#EC4899`):** Keyframe flags, render-complete pings, high-energy actions, and generative enhancement mode indicators.
- **Neutral Hierarchy:** 
  - Canvas & Panels: `#07050D` (Canvas), `#0F0C1B` (Surfaces), `#1B162E` (Inputs & Inactive Wells).
  - Borders & Hairlines: `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.18)` and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - Typography: Crisp White (`#F8FAFC`) for primary hierarchy, Muted Lavender-Gray (`#94A3B8`) for secondary metrics and prompts, and Deep Lavender-Slate (`#64748B`) for structural metadata.

## Typography

Typography strikes a dialogue between futuristic geometric authority and microscopic parameter legibility.

- **Headlines (Sora):** Sora’s geometric aperture and wide character construction give titles, model presets, and render alerts a definitive cinematic sheen. Headings are set tight with slight negative tracking to reinforce solid digital architecture.
- **Body & Metadata (Hanken Grotesk):** Hanken Grotesk acts as the utilitarian engine. Its neutral grotesque skeleton delivers maximum legibility across parameter inspectors, prompt engineering fields, aspect ratio matrices, and complex multi-layer timeline headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an integrated multi-pane workspace that collapses seamlessly from widescreen production monitors down to mobile review decks.

### Layout Philosophy
- **The Studio Grid:** Widescreen environments use a fixed-rail/fluid-canvas model. Side panels (Inspector, Prompt Hub, Asset Drawer) hold fixed structural widths, while the viewport canvas and multi-track sequence timeline scale dynamically to fill available horizontal and vertical real estate.
- **Rhythm Base:** Built on a rigorous 4px baseline (`0.25rem`), enabling compact, pixel-precise numeric inputs, timeline scrub notches, and dense node trees without visual clutter.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Reflow
- **Desktop (1440px+):** Full multi-pane interface: 4.5rem left tool rail, dynamic video monitor, 20rem right attribute inspector, and collapsible multi-track timeline spanning the lower half.
- **Tablet (768px – 1439px):** Inspector slides into an overlay flyout drawer. Canvas dominates top region; timeline condenses to active-layer view with modal track selectors.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** Single-column stacked workflow. Viewport preview pinned top, generative prompt console docked bottom, with swipeable tab bar toggling timeline frames, generation queues, and camera nodes. Margins default to 1rem (`space-md`).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through luminous glassmorphism and atmospheric obsidian layering, creating the optical illusion of stacked glass control panels illuminated from behind.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Base Surface (Ground 0):** Pure obsidian (`#07050D`). Serves as the infinite background behind nodes and preview viewports.
2. **Sub-Panels (Level 1):** Elevated dark tint (`#0F0C1B`) with 85% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, and a 1px perimeter border rendered in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
3. **Floating Modules & Menus (Level 2):** `#161226` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Enhanced with an ambient violet perimeter glow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 32px -4px rgba(7, 5, 13, 0.8), 0 0 0 1px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`).
4. **Active Modals & Render Overlays (Level 3):** Deep obsidian glass with a radiant cyan-to-violet rim light: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9), 0 0 20px -2px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`.

### Border Lighting Technique
Avoid opaque solid borders. Use hairline strokes (`1px`) driven by radial gradients that simulate light catching the top-left edge of dark acrylic glass panels (`border-image: linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.15), rgba(139,92,246,0.15), transparent)`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces a calibrated `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base radius). This produces tailored corners that preserve the mechanical precision expected of professional media suites without feeling sharp or abrasive.

### Shape Geometry Rules
- **Micro UI & Inputs (`rounded-md` / `0.5rem`):** Number counters, prompt token inputs, scrub nodes, and parameter dropdowns maintain balanced 8px radii.
- **Structural Cards & Panels (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl` / `1rem` to `1.5rem`):** Studio docking bays, video preview containers, and modal dialogs adopt sweeping 16px to 24px radii to frame generated visual content gracefully.
- **Interactive Badges & Status Indicators (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Status badges, frame rate counters, camera zoom pills, and quick-filter switches use continuous pill caps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Glowing Action (Generate / Render / Create / Approve):** Linear gradient from vibrant neon purple (`#A855F7`, `purple-500`) to electric cyan (`#06B6D4`, `cyan-500`) with hover states shifting to (`#C084FC`, `purple-400`) and (`#22D3EE`, `cyan-400`), set in high-contrast obsidian slate text (`#020617`, `text-slate-950 font-bold`), with an ambient neon glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.25)` expanding on hover).
- **Secondary Action (Export / Branch Prompt):** Transparent obsidian body (`rgba(255,255,255,0.03)`), framed with a 1px cyan hairline border (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`). Text in `#06B6D4`. Hover transitions border to solid `#06B6D4` with a subtle inner azure sheen.
- **Ghost / Tool Buttons:** 0.5rem rounded icon toggles with muted lavender `#94A3B8` icons, transitioning to `#F8FAFC` with an active background of `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`.

### Status Badges & Pills
- Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`) with a micro font size (`label-sm`).
- **Rendering State:** Background `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`, text `#C4B5FD`, border `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)`, anchored by a pulsating neon purple dot.
- **Real-Time / Live State:** Background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`, text `#67E8F9`, border `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`.
- **Model Training / Queue:** Background `rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.1)`, text `#F472B6`, border `rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.3)`.

### Input Fields & Prompt Consoles
- **Prompt Area:** Dark crystalline input well (`#0B0816`) bounded by `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`. Focus shifts border instantly to gradient purple-to-cyan accompanied by a soft interior glow (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 12px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`). Text renders in high-contrast `#F8FAFC`, placeholder in muted `#64748B`.
- **Numeric Scrub Inputs (FPS, Resolution, Seed):** Compact dark chips featuring monospace-aligned digits. Hover displays vertical scrub arrows; dragging horizontally illuminates a cyan trail.

### Glassmorphic Cards & Video Nodes
- **Render Output Card:** Translucent base `#0F0C1B` (90% opacity), `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, with a subtle 1px border. Hover invokes an animated violet-magenta border gradient shimmer and elevates the card slightly along the Z-axis.
- Top metadata bar houses a pill badge and timecode tracker; bottom overlays display prompt weight parameters on hover.

### Checkboxes, Toggles & Sliders
- **Checkboxes:** Squared with `0.25rem` radius, deep obsidian background with an active violet fill and crisp white check glyph.
- **Progress Lines & Step Indicators:** Horizontally filled tracks rendered in `bg-gradient-to-r from-purple-500 to-cyan-400` with subtle violet bloom `shadow-[0_0_10px_rgba(168,85,247,0.3)]` on dark obsidian hairline tracks.
- **Sliders (CFG Scale, Motion Strength, Denoise):** 4px thick track in dark slate (`#1E1A33`), filled track rendered in a continuous cyan-to-purple gradient. Slider thumb is a glowing 14px white circle encircled by an active `#8B5CF6` halo ring.

### Domain-Specific Suite Additions
- **Timeline Keyframe Track:** Horizontally scrolling slate lanes with neon cyan scrubber heads and vibrant magenta diamond keyframe markers. Hovering over keyframe markers triggers floating miniature thumbnail tooltips wrapped in frosted glass.
- **Generation Progress Arc:** Dual-concentric ring loaders displaying active diffusion step counts, pulsing from violet to cyan as inference resolves.
